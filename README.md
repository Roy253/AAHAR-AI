**AAHAR AI — AI-Powered Hostel Nutrition & Meal Planning Assistant**

AAHAR AI is an AI-powered nutrition and meal-planning application designed to help college students make healthier food choices while living in hostels. It focuses on a common problem: hostel mess meals may not always meet students' nutritional needs, while limited budgets make it difficult to find affordable alternatives.

Built with Python and Streamlit, AAHAR AI combines nutrition analysis, hostel mess food tracking, budget-conscious food recommendations, and a conversational AI assistant powered by a locally running Ollama LLM. The project aims to connect nutrition data with practical, personalized suggestions that students can use in their daily lives.

**Key Features**
- **Hostel Mess Food Tracking:** Log common meals such as rice, dal, roti, sabzi, and other foods with portion sizes.
- **Nutrition Gap Analysis:** Estimate daily calorie, protein, and fibre intake against configurable targets.
- **Budget-Friendly Recommendations:** Suggest affordable food additions based on nutritional gaps and dietary preferences.
- **AI Nutrition Chatbot:** Use a local LLM to provide conversational guidance and explain food recommendations.
- **Nutrition Data Integration:** Work with Indian Food Composition Table (IFCT) data and optional USDA FoodData Central lookups.
- **Meal Planning and Analysis:** Support meal-wise tracking, daily nutrition summaries, and data exports.

**Tech Stack:** Python, Streamlit, Pandas, Ollama, Local LLMs, IFCT Dataset, USDA FoodData Central API.

**Project Goal:** Build a practical, accessible AI nutrition assistant that helps students understand their eating habits and make more informed, budget-conscious food choices.

*Note: Nutrition values and recommendations are estimates and should not be treated as medical advice.*
